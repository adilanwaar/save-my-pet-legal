Privacy Policy

Fitness Quest - Save My Pet · Effective Date: October 29, 2026

Your Fitness Belongs to You: We do not track your GPS location, we do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your physical activity or health data for advertising.

1. Introduction

Fitness Quest - Save My Pet ("we", "our", or "us") provides a fitness habit gamification platform where your daily physical activity feeds, nurtures, and cares for your virtual pet. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account Information

When you register an account, we collect your email address and securely hashed password. If you specify a nickname or display name, we store this to represent you within private friend circles.

B. Android Health Connect & Activity Data

With your explicit opt-in consent, our app connects to Android Health Connect to read:

  • Daily Steps (READ_STEPS): Used to calculate Care Points (CP) to feed your pet.
  • Active Calories Burned (READ_ACTIVE_CALORIES_BURNED): Used to convert non-step workouts (swimming, cycling, yoga) into Care Points at a rate of 20 CP per active kcal burned.
  • Exercise Sessions (READ_EXERCISE): Used to prevent double-counting between walking intervals and workouts.

C. Offline Local Storage

To enable reliable offline functionality, daily step summaries and pet vitals are cached locally on your device within sandboxed application storage.

3. Compliance with Google Play Health Connect Policy

We strictly comply with Google Play's User Data Policy and Health Connect requirements:

  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or transfer Health Connect data to data brokers, ad networks, or third parties.
  • Zero Advertising Use: We never use Health Connect data to serve ads or conduct advertising user profiling.
  • Purpose-Specific Use: Activity data is processed solely to calculate in-app virtual pet vitals and display your personal fitness ledger.
  • User Revocation: You can revoke Health Connect access at any time through the app's Profile settings or your device system settings.
  • Non-Medical Disclaimer: This app is strictly for general wellness and fitness motivation. It is not a medical device and provides no medical diagnosis or treatment.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell personal data. Data is processed exclusively by:

  • Secure Cloud Infrastructure: Cloud hosting adhering to ISO 27001 / SOC 2 compliance for user authentication and state synchronization.
  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store payment details.

5. Security and Encryption

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. User passwords are encrypted with bcrypt, and internal tokens are cryptographically signed with expiration lifecycles.

6. Account Deletion & Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You maintain complete ownership of your data:

  • In-App Deletion: Tap Profile > Privacy & Account > Delete Account & Purge Data. All user records, pet history, and health ledgers are permanently purged immediately.
  • Data Portability: Tap Export Personal Data to download a JSON file of your complete account history.
  • Manual Request: Contact support@savemypet.app with "Delete My Account".
